Who We Are

Built on Five Pillars

Mr & Miss Maboneng exists to do more than crown a winner — every pillar shapes how contestants grow long after the final walk.

🎯

Mission

To give young men and women a stage worthy of their ambition — where confidence, character, and culture are celebrated as loudly as beauty.

👁

Vision

A Maboneng where every contestant leaves more self-assured than they arrived, carrying a title that opens doors long after the final walk.

✨

Youth Empowerment

Workshops and mentorship built for young people stepping into their own spotlight for the very first time.

👑

Leadership

Titleholders represent Maboneng year-round, trained to lead with purpose and drive initiatives that outlast the crown.

🎭

Talent Development

Every contestant coached by industry mentors who sharpen raw talent into stage-ready craft.

The Road to the Crown

Event Details

From open registrations to the live finale — here is how the season unfolds.

1
Phase One

Open Registrations

Open call across Johannesburg. Walk-ins welcome, online registration opens first.

2
Phase Two

Bootcamp & Training

Weeks of runway coaching, public speaking and styling with industry mentors.

3
Phase Three

Meet & Greet

Finalists introduced to the public at a Maboneng precinct showcase.

4
Phase Four

Finale Night

The crowning of Mr & Miss Maboneng, live at Ellis Park Stadium, Johannesburg.
